Your library and settings

Hanami is a watch-list and episode tracker. It does not provide video streaming. No Hanami account, advertising, advertising identifier, or developer-operated analytics service is used.

Saved titles, episode progress, ratings, notes, reminders, recommendation dismissals, shared source links and preferences are kept in private app storage. The developer has no remote access to this storage. Catalogue lookups and optional services described below send some information off the device. Third-party SDK diagnostics also apply, so Hanami does not claim that no data is collected.

Catalogue searches and recommendations

Hanami sends search terms, including likely title names extracted from shared text or images, to The Movie Database (TMDB). Browsing, recommendations, details and background refreshes send catalogue IDs and filters. Poster requests go to TMDB's image servers. These requests can disclose which titles interest you. Private notes, ratings and progress records are not uploaded to TMDB.

Catalogue details are refreshed periodically, typically daily for airing or upcoming titles and about monthly otherwise when connectivity and Android scheduling allow. Responses and artwork may be cached locally. Providers receive IP addresses, request times and client information, and may keep logs under their policies. Hanami does not control their retention or promise memory-only processing.

Optional episode tracking and Notification access

Notification access is optional. Hanami explains it before opening Android's access settings. Android exposes active media sessions and notifications when access is granted. Hanami examines media titles, episode information, duration and playback position locally to offer app tracking and update progress for selected apps. For selected apps without media controls, it can read the title and text of an ongoing playback notification. It does not use message notifications for advertising or upload notification contents to the developer.

Media information is held temporarily in memory. The app remembers selected and ignored apps and resulting library changes, and shows discovered players in its tracking screen. Unselected players can trigger an invitation to track them. Decline an app, disable individual tracked apps, or revoke Notification access to stop tracking. If you choose to search for a detected title, that search goes to TMDB. Related-title recommendations can request catalogue IDs.

Shared links, pictures and videos

Shared links can request public previews from YouTube, TikTok, X or the linked website. That provider receives the URL and network information. Instagram, Facebook and Threads links are not intentionally fetched directly; Hanami uses supplied captions. Other websites control their redirects and logging. Only HTTPS requests are allowed within Hanami.

Pictures and extracted video frames are read locally with Google's bundled ML Kit text-recognition model. Image pixels and recognized text are not sent to Google by that recognition API. Likely title names can subsequently be searched with TMDB. ML Kit diagnostics are described below.

Anime scene matching is a separate opt-in. After you agree, Hanami can upload a reduced image or one or two extracted stills to trace.moe for identification. The service also receives your IP address and request information. Do not enable this for private images you do not want to send. You can decline and use text recognition, or turn scene matching off in Settings. Hanami cannot guarantee trace.moe's retention of request information.

Shared originals are not added to your media library. Processing uses memory and temporary cache files. Public previews and a still prepared for an external image-search app may remain cached until replaced, cleared by Android, or removed by clearing Hanami's cache. Choosing Google Lens or another app passes the selected image through Android's sharing mechanism; that app's privacy terms apply.

Google SDK diagnostics and updates

ML Kit can send Google device/app information, a per-installation identifier, feature-use events, error codes and performance measurements. Metrics can include image format, dimensions and input/output sizes, but not image contents or recognized text. Google uses these for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. Bundling the model allows local recognition; it does not disable metrics. ML Kit may contact Google for compatibility information and updates. Google's policies govern its collection and retention.

Google Play's in-app update service checks for and installs available updates through the Play Store under Google's terms. Hanami does not receive your Google account credentials.

Crash reports and support

Hanami keeps the latest crash report privately, excluded from backups. It may contain app version, device model, Android version, error stack traces and recent app warnings. It does not intentionally include your library, but errors may contain contextual information. The configured TMDB credential is redacted. Review reports before sending.

Nothing is automatically emailed. Report a problem or the crash prompt opens a draft in your mail or sharing app that you can inspect and cancel. The recipient receives the report, your added text and sender/contact information provided by your selected service. Support messages are used to respond and diagnose problems. Contact narzo9990@gmail.com to request deletion of correspondence held by the developer. Your messaging provider manages its own copies and retention.

Permissions and security

Internet and network-state access support catalogue and image requests. Notification permission supports reminders, release alerts and tracking notices. Hanami asks when you enable notification features; the app remains usable if you decline. Optional Notification access supports episode tracking as described above.

Android WorkManager uses wake-lock and boot-completed permissions to run and reschedule work. No exact-alarm, foreground-service, location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call-log, broad media/storage, Accessibility, or all-installed-packages permission is requested. Files arrive through user-selected share grants. Launcher-app visibility provides local player labels and icons; Hanami does not upload an installed-app inventory.

App-managed network traffic uses HTTPS. Local information is protected by Android's app sandbox and device security. No system guarantees absolute security.

Backup, retention and deletion

With Android backup enabled, the watch-list database can be included in encrypted cloud backup or supported device transfer. This includes notes, progress and saved source links. The developer cannot access your Android backup. Settings, scene-upload consent, tracked-app preferences, SDK identifiers, caches, crash reports and work queues are excluded from Hanami's backup allowlist. Android controls backup availability and restoration.

Library records remain until removed or app storage is cleared. Settings and recommendation dismissals remain until changed or storage is cleared. Later crashes replace old reports. Caches are removed by eviction or clearing cache.

To remove all locally held data, open Settings → Manage local data, then Android's Storage / Clear storage control. Uninstalling also removes local app storage. These actions do not remove Android backups or messages already sent; manage those with your backup or messaging provider. A later Android restore may restore a backed-up library. There is no Hanami account to delete.

Audience and changes

Hanami is for teenagers and adults, not children under 13. Catalogue entries can contain mature themes even though adult-search results are excluded. External services have their own age and usage requirements.

Changed data practices will be reflected in this policy and applicable in-app disclosures. Contact narzo9990@gmail.com for privacy questions or deletion requests for support correspondence.
